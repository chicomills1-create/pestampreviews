Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PE stamp?

A PE stamp is a licensed professional engineer's seal applied to drawings the PE has reviewed under responsible charge — the mark a building department requires before a permit issues. A legitimate seal is never a product on a shelf: it follows a real review by the engineer who will stand behind the work.

Can you buy a PE stamp online?

No — not legitimately. A seal requires PE review under responsible charge; bare-stamp sales are not legitimate, and any provider offering one is offering something a permit office should not accept. What you can start online is the review itself: send finished drawings, a licensed PE reviews them under responsible charge, corrections come back if the set needs them, and the seal is applied when the responsible PE approves.

How long does review take?

Timing follows scope — the drawing type, the disciplines and states involved, and how much correction a set needs before a PE can responsibly seal it — and this desk states timing honestly, without invented guarantees. The standard the providers on this page publish is the family's 48-Hour Review Start: when review begins, stated as a commitment a buyer can hold them to.

What does a PE stamp cost?

It depends on scope — drawing type, disciplines, states, and corrections. Any provider quoting a flat stamp price without seeing your drawings is guessing, because the review is the product: a clean, complete set and a set that needs engineering work are different jobs. Send the set and get a scoped answer instead.
